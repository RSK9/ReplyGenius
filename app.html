Quick tips
For 1-2 star reviews

Always apologise first. Acknowledge the specific issue. Invite them back. Never argue or make excuses.

For 5 star reviews

Thank them by name if possible. Mention something specific from their review. Invite them back.

Response speed matters

Businesses that reply within 24 hours get 33% more future reviews. Reply fast, every time.
